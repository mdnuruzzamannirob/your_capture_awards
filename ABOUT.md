About Your Capture Awards

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Overview

Your Capture Awards is a global online community and contest platform for photographers of all skill levels. We celebrate creativity, craft, and storytelling through photography by running curated contests, recognizing outstanding work with achievement badges, and building meaningful engagement between photographers, judges, and audiences.

Our Mission

- Inspire photographers to push their creative boundaries.
- Recognize and reward excellence in photographic storytelling.
- Build a welcoming, transparent, and fair platform where photographers can showcase work, learn, and connect.

What We Do

- Host regular themed and open photography contests judged by experts and community voting.
- Award achievement badges and levels to recognize top performers and milestones.
- Provide tools for photographers to upload, present, and promote their work safely and professionally.

Core Values

- Integrity: We run contests transparently and fairly.
- Respect: We treat every creator and participant with dignity.
- Excellence: We champion outstanding technical and artistic achievement.
- Community: We foster collaboration, feedback, and growth among photographers.
